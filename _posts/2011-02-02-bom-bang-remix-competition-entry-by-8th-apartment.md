I entered the February 2011 remix competition for Marco Bailey’s “Bom Bang!” under my artist name **8th Apartment**. Organized by Bedrock Records and Marco Bailey, the competition offered a shared source track for producers to reinterpret.

This recording is my submitted remix, preserved here alongside the other music projects in the archive.
