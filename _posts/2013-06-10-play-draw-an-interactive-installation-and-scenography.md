“Play + Draw” was an interactive installation and scenography project at the 2013 [FIGMENT Festival on Governors Island, New York](https://newyork.figmentproject.org). Visitors could take on the roles of VJ and DJ, manipulating sound and fields of video texture through the installation’s controls. Their actions were also streamed online.

The project brought together digital video specialist and VJ Miixxy Melissa Ulto, Mark Alan Johnson (VJ DoctorMojo), and me as the founder of VJs Magazine.

Mojo Video Tech Inc. provided technical direction, VDMX programming, video mapping and projection system design. Those systems connected visitors’ actions to the shared audiovisual space.

[Hyperallergic’s coverage](https://hyperallergic.com/73036/social-media-at-the-helm-of-performance-on-governors-island/) provides additional context for the project and festival.
