I filmed, edited and animated this report from the 2012 Mapping Festival in Geneva. It brings together live performances and behind-the-scenes footage, with Rory Phillips’ “Chamber Music (Original Mix)” as the soundtrack.

The film was presented through VJs TV, the platform I launched to collect and share work by VJs and video artists. That connection mattered: the report could introduce viewers to the festival, then point them toward a broader archive of audiovisual work.

At the time, VJs TV combined a searchable video collection with an uninterrupted viewing channel. The submission invitation at the end of the film belongs to that original project period; the video remains a record of the festival and the platform’s early editorial direction.
