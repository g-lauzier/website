This project follows R3hab at the 2012 Montreux Jazz Festival and at MAD Club in Lausanne the following night. I combined performance footage, crowd shots and behind-the-scenes moments into a film for Noctune.

## Filming and movement

I used a steadicam to move through the venues and capture the performance without relying only on fixed stage views. Those moving shots provide a connection between the artist, the crowd and the surrounding space.

## Animation and finishing

Alongside the live footage, I created a 3D avatar of R3hab in Cinema 4D. The animation adds a separate graphic element to the film, bringing the artist’s identity into the edit beyond the concert footage itself.

I also edited and color-corrected the material. The finished piece brings the two nights together while retaining the different lighting and atmosphere of the festival and club settings.
