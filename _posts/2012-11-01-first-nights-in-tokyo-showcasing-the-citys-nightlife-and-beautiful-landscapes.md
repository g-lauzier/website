I filmed “First Nights in Tokyo” during the winter of 2012 on a Canon 5D Mark II. The film moves between snowy Shibuya, Tokyo Tower, the temples of Asakusa, and the streets of Omotesando and Harajuku.

Nightlife scenes include The Quest in Roppongi. Rather than a complete city guide, the video is a sequence of places and impressions from that trip, set to The Subs’ “Don’t Stop” (Autoerotique remix).
