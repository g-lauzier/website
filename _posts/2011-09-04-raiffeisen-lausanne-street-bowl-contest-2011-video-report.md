I filmed and edited this report of the Raiffeisen Lausanne Street & Bowl Contest, organized by LSBC in Lausanne from September 1–4, 2011. The event took place at Vidy and HS36.

The film follows the competition and includes the results for the PROS, GIRLS and AMATEURS categories, using the event’s original category names. It was produced in 1280 × 720 HD, with Quantic’s “Apricot Morning” as the soundtrack.

**Filming and editing:** Guillaume J. Lauzier.
