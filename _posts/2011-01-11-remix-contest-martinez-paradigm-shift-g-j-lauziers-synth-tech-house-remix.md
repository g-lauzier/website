In January 2011, I made a synth tech house remix of Martinez’s “Paradigm Shift” for the contest hosted by Moon Harbour and Juno. I worked with the supplied track parts in Ableton and Logic Pro, developing my own arrangement around the original material.

The competition invited participants to submit their remixes by February 15. My entry is credited to G.J. Lauzier in the recording below.
