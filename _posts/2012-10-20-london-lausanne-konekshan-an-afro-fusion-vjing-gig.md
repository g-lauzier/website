I created this teaser for my VJ performance at London Lausanne Konekshan in Lausanne in 2012. The visual approach combined footage of dance and everyday movement with contrasting effects and layers of color.

The material was treated as a basis for audiovisual composition: movement, rhythm and repetition connect the images to the music. The soundtrack is Zepherin Saint’s “Love Of My Life (Atjazz Astro Dub).”

The teaser pairs dance imagery with electronic music, using color and effects to emphasize movement. It documents my approach to live visuals during that period.
