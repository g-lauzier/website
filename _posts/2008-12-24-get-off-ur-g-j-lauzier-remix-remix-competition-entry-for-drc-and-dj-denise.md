I produced an electro house remix of DRC and DJ Denise’s “Get Off Ur” for a remix competition associated with Mizumo Music. Working from the organizers’ supplied samples, I built the track in Ableton and Logic Pro and released it under my name, G.J. Lauzier.

The recording below preserves that entry in my music archive.
