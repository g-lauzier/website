I launched GVA PoGo in 2017 as a live map for Pokémon GO players in Geneva. It brought together Pokémon sightings, PokéStops, nests and gyms, with filters to help players find the information relevant to them. The project’s feature set also included raid information, lure details and notifications through Twitter and Discord.

## Organizing a changing map

The application used Python and JavaScript with a MySQL database. Independent worker threads collected and processed location information across the Geneva area, while the interface made those records available on a multilingual map.

The map depended on the coverage and freshness of its observations. Filters and notifications helped users navigate the information that was available.

The feature set developed over the life of the project. The archived images below record different views of the application.
